The work

GALLERIES

These portfolios have been built up over almost 8 years of capturing the night and world around me. You'll find no AI-generated art here. All of my images have been created using various techniques and processes, skills I have spent my whole photography journey learning. Some use complex long-exposure photographs, to reveal much more color and detail in the night sky than the human eye can see — others are simple, a quick snapshot of the world around me at the time.
